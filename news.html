17 April 2024
Academia
Phys.org featured our Floquet Fermi Liquid work Our PRL paper on Floquet Fermi liquids was featured by Phys.org.

The article, "Beyond equilibrium: Scientists investigate Floquet Fermi liquids", discusses our work on a nonequilibrium state that can emerge when a Fermi liquid is periodically driven while coupled to a fermionic bath. In this state, the occupation does not simply become a hotter Fermi-Dirac distribution. Instead, the driven system can develop a staircase-like structure with multiple nested Floquet Fermi surfaces.

The feature also highlights how these nested surfaces can affect quantum oscillations and how driven electronic states may provide routes toward controlling electronic behavior with light.
