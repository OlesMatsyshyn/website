About Oles Matsyshyn

Short Biography

I am a theoretical physicist and systems developer working across quantum materials, nonequilibrium dynamics, nonlinear responses, superconductivity, and computational modeling. I am currently a Research Fellow in the Materials Science group at the School of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

My scientific work combines analytical theory with large-scale numerical simulation. I build models and computational tools for quantum geometry, light-matter interaction, Floquet and open quantum systems, superconducting response, and driven electronic materials. My current direction is to connect quantum materials with quantum-information-inspired ways of storing, processing, and transforming information.

Beyond academic research, I develop quantitative systems, trading infrastructure, data-analysis tools, public-outreach material, and the dark strategy-survival game Vyrva.
