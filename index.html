Personal website

Oles Matsyshyn

Physicist | Quantitative Systems Developer | Systems Architect

I study and build complex systems that evolve, respond, and organize: quantum electrons in materials, noisy financial markets, and simulated worlds shaped by interacting agents, environments, and rules. Across these areas, my interest is the same: explore, identify what is unclear, make it clear, and look for even more hidden structures.

Different systems, same question: what is the hidden structure behind the motion?

Primary professional work

My primary professional work is in physics, quantitative systems, and scientific software. Vyrva and Public Outreach document independent creative and educational projects developed alongside that work.
